04 RESEARCH NOTE

When does a color become the car's?颜色，究竟属于谁？

A diffusion model can draw every requested object and color, then assign the colors to the wrong objects. Counterfactual prompt switches offer a way to study when that assignment changes.扩散模型可能画对对象和颜色，却配错归属。反事实提示词切换提供了研究归属何时还能改变的方法。
