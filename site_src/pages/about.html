I care about the parts of AI systems that are easy to overlook.我关心 AI 系统里最容易被忽略的部分。

I'm pursuing a master's degree in Computer Application Technology at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, through UCAS. I also work as an LLM algorithm intern at JD Explore Academy.我在中国科学院大学攻读计算机应用技术硕士，培养单位为中国科学院自动化研究所；同时在京东探索研究院担任大模型算法实习生。

My research spans generative models, multimodal learning, post-training, and agents. I ask when an image model commits to an object's attribute, whether a token backbone can read external features, which evidence an agent retains, and how a result can be traced back to a real run. The common thread is information flow: what a model receives, what it preserves, and what finally shapes its behavior.我的研究横跨生成模型、多模态学习、后训练与智能体。我关心图像模型何时确定属性归属、token 骨干能否读懂外部特征、智能体保留了哪些证据，以及一个结果能否追溯到真实执行过程。贯穿其中的是信息流：模型接收什么、保留什么，最终又是什么塑造了它的行为。

Before that, I studied Information and Computing Science at Huazhong Agricultural University, graduating first in my cohort. Applied work in computer vision and scientific forecasting taught me to start with the real operating conditions, not only the model architecture.本科就读于华中农业大学信息与计算科学专业，以专业第一毕业。早期在计算机视觉与科学预测上的应用工作，让我习惯先看真实使用条件，再谈模型结构。
